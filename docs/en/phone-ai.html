What else the AI does for you

  • Knows your regularsWhen a customer calls again, the AI knows their name, address and last order.
  • A second chance after a call without an orderSomeone hangs up without ordering? They get a WhatsApp message to order after all.
  • Checks the addressThe AI knows the streets per postcode, your delivery zones and the minimum order.
  • Transfers to your teamIf the caller wants to speak to someone, the AI puts them through and you get an alert.
  • Add to the order in the same callCustomer forgot something? The kitchen gets a separate ticket with what was added.
  • Answering machine outside opening hoursClosed? The AI still picks up and says when you open again.
  • A safety net for every orderIf the customer hangs up too early, the order still goes to the kitchen. After each call an AI checks that the ticket is complete.

FAQ

Questions restaurant owners ask

Does the AI sound natural?

The AI talks in normal sentences, understands questions about your menu and takes the order calmly. Book a demo and hear it yourself.

How many customers can call at the same time?

More than 50. The AI handles every call separately, so nobody waits or gets a busy tone, even during your busiest hours.

What do the minutes in the price mean?

That's the talk time the AI spends on the phone with your customers each month. Start has 500 minutes, Plus 1,000 and Pro 2,000.

Where does the order arrive?

In your Conveasy dashboard and on your kitchen printer, just like a WhatsApp or webshop order.

Do I need to replace my till?

No. Conveasy works separately from your till.

Can the AI take reservations too?

Yes. With the phone AI and the WhatsApp AI, customers book a table 24/7. The AI notes name, date, time and party size and puts the reservation in your dashboard. Reservations are included in the price, at no extra cost.
