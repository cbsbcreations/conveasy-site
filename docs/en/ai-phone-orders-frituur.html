Which AI takes phone orders for a Belgian frituur?

Conveasy's phone AI is made for it. It takes the complete frituur order by phone, with your own menu and prices, and sends it to the kitchen.

Book a demoSee the prices

A frituur order is full of choices: small or large fries, which sauce, which snacks, a drink, pickup or delivery. The AI asks for each of them, the way your staff would.

  • Your own menu: sizes, sauces, extras and menu deals.
  • Delivery zones by postcode and street, with the minimum order and delivery cost.
  • Several calls at the same time during the Friday rush.
  • The ticket prints in the kitchen, so nobody has to type the order over.

It runs at Eatpoint in Heusden-Zolder and City Food Lounge in Genk. The phone AI costs from €249 a month for 500 minutes. More on the page for frituren.
