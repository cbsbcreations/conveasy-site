FAQ

Questions restaurant owners ask

Is it really AI or a button menu?

It's AI. Customers just type what they want, as they would to a member of staff, and the AI understands.

Does it work on my own number?

Yes, customers order through your restaurant's WhatsApp number.

What does it cost?

€99 a month, plus a one-time setup fee of €199.

Do I pay commission per order?

No. You pay a fixed price, no commission.

Can the AI take reservations too?

Yes. With the phone AI and the WhatsApp AI, customers book a table 24/7. The AI notes name, date, time and party size and puts the reservation in your dashboard. Reservations are included in the price, at no extra cost.
