AI phone assistants for restaurants in Belgium, compared

Not every AI phone assistant does the same job. Some book tables, some are a phone system with AI added, some are platforms to build your own agent, and a few take complete food orders. Here are the types, the providers you will come across in Belgium and what each suits best.

Written by Conveasy. Facts about other providers come from their own websites or press about them, checked on 19 November 2026.

First decide what the AI must do

  • Orders or reservations? Taking a food order with sizes, sauces and a delivery address is a different job from booking a table.
  • Your till: some assistants work inside a reservation system or connect to a POS, others work on their own.
  • Languages: in Belgium callers speak Dutch and French, and often English or Turkish.
  • Price: is it published, and is it per minute, per user or per month?

When Conveasy is the right choice

Choose Conveasy if most of your calls are orders for pickup or delivery, you want the order in the kitchen without a POS integration, and you want published prices. It also takes table reservations and WhatsApp orders.

When another option fits better

  • You only take table reservations and already use Zenchef: Zenchef Call AI works inside that software.
  • You need a phone system for a whole team, with routing and queues: look at a phone system with AI such as CloudTalk or Dstny.
  • You have developers or an agency and want to build everything yourself: a platform such as Retell AI gives you the most freedom.

FAQ

Questions restaurant owners ask

What is the best AI phone assistant for a takeaway restaurant in Belgium?

For takeaway and delivery, pick an assistant that takes the complete order from your menu, checks delivery addresses and sends the order to the kitchen. Conveasy is built for that and runs at restaurants in Heusden-Zolder and Genk; fonio.ai also takes orders. Reservation assistants such as Zenchef Call AI focus on booking tables.

Which AI phone assistants work without a POS?

Conveasy works separately from the till and prints its own kitchen ticket. Retell AI can be connected to a POS, and Zenchef Call AI works inside the Zenchef reservation software.

Are the prices published?

Conveasy publishes its prices: from €249 a month for 500 minutes. CloudTalk lists €25 per user per month excluding AI add-ons, and Retell AI works pay-as-you-go. For the others, see their websites.
