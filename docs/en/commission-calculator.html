FAQ

Questions restaurant owners ask

How are the savings worked out?

Orders per month × average order value × the platform's commission percentage gives the commission you pay now. Take off €79 for your own webshop. The rest is what you save each month.

What does Conveasy cost instead?

A fixed price per month, with 0% commission on orders: webshop €79, WhatsApp AI €99, phone AI from €249. Plus a one-time setup fee of €199 per channel (€299 for the phone AI).

Do I have to leave the platforms?

No. You can stay on the platforms and take orders through your own channels as well. Every order that comes in through your own webshop, WhatsApp or phone has no commission.
