Made for the rush in your restaurant

You know the feeling: the phone rings while the fryer is full. Conveasy takes those orders and reservations for you, so you can cook.

Why Conveasy

During the rush, phone orders get lost, and platforms take a big share of every order. Conveasy answers the phone, replies on WhatsApp and gives you your own webshop and kiosk.

One system for everything

All orders and reservations land in one dashboard and on your kitchen screen or printer, whatever the channel.

Direct orders first

No commission on your own channels. Customers order from you, not through a platform.
