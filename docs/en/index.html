FAQ

Questions restaurant owners ask

What is Conveasy?

Conveasy is AI ordering software for restaurants in the Benelux. An AI takes orders on the phone and WhatsApp, and you get your own webshop with QR ordering, a self-order kiosk and Marketing AI with a digital stamp card. All orders land in one dashboard and print in the kitchen.

Where do my orders arrive?

In your Conveasy dashboard and on a thermal printer in your kitchen.

Do I need to replace my till?

No. Conveasy works separately from your till.

Do I pay commission per order?

No. You pay a fixed monthly price.

Which languages does the AI speak?

More than 60. The AI replies in the customer's language.

How fast can I start?

You send your menu and we set everything up. You're live within 10 days at most.

Can I take channels separately?

Yes. Phone, WhatsApp, webshop and kiosk are each available on their own.

Can the AI take reservations too?

Yes. With the phone AI and the WhatsApp AI, customers book a table 24/7. The AI notes name, date, time and party size and puts the reservation in your dashboard. Reservations are included in the price, at no extra cost.
