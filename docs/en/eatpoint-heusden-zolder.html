How to order at Eatpoint

Call 011 43 24 10

A Conveasy AI assistant answers straight away, knows the menu and the prices, checks your delivery address and sends your order to the kitchen. It recognises regular customers by their number. Outside opening hours it tells you when the restaurant opens again.

WhatsApp 0467 05 16 61

Send a message. The WhatsApp AI takes your order in the chat, works out the price and the delivery cost and lets you confirm with one tap. Then the order goes to the kitchen.

Online: eatpoint.be

In Eatpoint's own webshop you order for pickup or delivery. The webshop runs on Conveasy, with no commission for the restaurant.
