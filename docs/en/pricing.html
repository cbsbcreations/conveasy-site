FAQ

Questions restaurant owners ask

How fast can I start?

You send your menu and we set everything up. You're live within 10 days at most.

No. You pay a fixed monthly price, however many orders you get.

Yes. Phone, WhatsApp, webshop and kiosk are each available on their own.

What if I need more minutes?

When your plan's minutes run out, you move up to a bigger plan.
